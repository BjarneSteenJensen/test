## Books
- **An Autobiography of Trauma: A Healing Journey** (Sacred Planet, 2023): Levine's memoir remains the most discussed of his titles in current reviews. It recounts a childhood sexual assault and how it shaped his development of Somatic Experiencing, interwoven with reflections on his own healing process. A Kirkus Reviews assessment calls it "a powerfully transparent, knowledge-drenched, and generous invitation" into his personal world, while noting some uneven transitions between anecdotes; a practitioner review (Confident Change Management) says it may not be his best-written book but is his "most inspiring" for SE practitioners since it braids his personal story with the origin story of the method. Source: [Kirkus Reviews](https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/peter-a-levine/an-autobiography-of-trauma/), [Confident Change Management review](https://confidentchangemanagement.com/book-reviews/psychology-neurology-philosophy/trauma/autobiography-of-trauma/), [Simon & Schuster publisher page](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/An-Autobiography-of-Trauma/Peter-A-Levine/9798888500767)
