## Books
- **An Autobiography of Trauma: A Healing Journey (Park Street Press, 2024)**: Levine's memoir continues to draw commentary — Kirkus Reviews calls it "beautifully written" with painful, personal detail, while Publishers Weekly was more critical, noting it "is pitched too uneasily between self-promotion and self-examination." The book recounts his childhood trauma and the origins of Somatic Experiencing, with cover endorsements from Alanis Morissette and Bessel van der Kolk. It remains the anchor for his current conference appearances (see above). Source: https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/peter-a-levine/an-autobiography-of-trauma/ and https://www.publishersweekly.com/9798888500767
