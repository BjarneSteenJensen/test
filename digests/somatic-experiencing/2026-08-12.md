## Books
- **Reviews of "Waking the Tiger" continue to appear**: Therapist Annie Wright published a review of Levine's foundational book *Waking the Tiger: Healing Trauma* (with Ann Frederick), examining what the book gets right about trauma living in the body while cautioning that "the mind still needs a voice in the healing" — a critique of over-applying a body-only framing. The piece was published in spring 2026 and appears to have been revisited/updated in July 2026. Source: https://anniewright.com/waking-the-tiger-review-a-therapists-take-on-somatic-healing/
- **"An Autobiography of Trauma: A Healing Journey" remains in circulation and discussion**: Levine's 2024 memoir (Park Street Press), in which he applies SE to his own childhood trauma, continues to surface in interviews and reviews rather than as new-release news. A New Books Network interview/episode and a published book review (International Body Psychotherapy Journal) both discuss it. Sources: https://newbooksnetwork.com/an-autobiography-of-trauma ; https://www.ciis.edu/podcast/peter-levine-autobiography-trauma
