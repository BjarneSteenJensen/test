## Books
- **An Autobiography of Trauma: A Healing Journey** (Park Street Press / Sacred Planet, released April 2, 2024) remains Levine's most recent book and continues to draw reviews and discussion. Kirkus Reviews calls it "a personal and revealing (if slightly scattered) memoir from a trailblazing therapist," noting it opens with a trigger warning as Levine discusses his own childhood trauma, including sexual assault, and how Somatic Experiencing helped him process it. Source: [Kirkus Reviews](https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/peter-a-levine/an-autobiography-of-trauma/), [New Books Network](https://newbooksnetwork.com/an-autobiography-of-trauma)
