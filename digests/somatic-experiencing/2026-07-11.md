- **An Autobiography of Trauma: A Healing Journey** (Sacred Planet/Inner Traditions, 2024): Levine's memoir remains his most recently published book and continues to draw reviews. Kirkus Reviews called it "a personal and revealing (if slightly scattered) memoir from a trailblazing therapist," while Publishers Weekly described it as a "stark if disjointed memoir" that is "pitched too uneasily between self-promotion and self-examination."
