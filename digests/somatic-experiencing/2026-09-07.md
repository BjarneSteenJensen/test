## Books
- **An Autobiography of Trauma: A Healing Journey** (Sacred Planet / Simon & Schuster, 2024) continues to generate discussion and interviews in 2026. It is a memoir in which Levine describes his own childhood trauma alongside the development of Somatic Experiencing, including accounts of dreams and visions — among them recurring "visitations" from Albert Einstein, whom he describes as a personal spirit guide — that he says shaped his life's work. Reader reviews on Goodreads are mixed regarding its autobiographical structure. Source: https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/196786498-an-autobiography-of-trauma and https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/An-Autobiography-of-Trauma/Peter-A-Levine/9798888500767
- **Waking the Tiger: Healing Trauma** (with Ann Frederick) continues to circulate in a newer Penguin Random House printing (ISBN 9798889843573) alongside the original 1997 edition, and remains his best-known and most widely translated book (into ~20 languages), still referenced heavily as the entry point to his work. No new 2026 edition or translation announcement was found beyond ongoing reprints. Source: https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/812919/waking-the-tiger-healing-trauma-by-peter-a-levine-phd-contributions-by-ann-frederick/
